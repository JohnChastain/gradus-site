One place for the practice itself

Gradus is designed for musicians who want more than a timer. It helps organize what you are working on, preserves the next step for each piece, and makes technical progress easier to review.

Recommendations remain editable, your recordings stay on your device, and supported practice records sync through your private iCloud database when iCloud is available.
