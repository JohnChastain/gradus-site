Gradus

Gradus is a practice app for musicians on iPhone and iPad. It plans practice sessions, keeps a metronome, timer and recorder on the practice screen, and tracks progress on repertoire and technique over time.

It has no accounts or ads, and your practice data stays on your device and in your own iCloud.
