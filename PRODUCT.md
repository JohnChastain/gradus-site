# Gradus Website

## Register

brand

## Users

Musicians evaluating Gradus, beta testers looking for help, and people who need
to understand how Gradus handles their practice data.

## Product Purpose

Provide a trustworthy public home for Gradus, its privacy policy, support
guidance, and required App Store links. The site should explain the product
without promising unproven learning outcomes.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, and musically serious.

## Anti-references

- Loud growth-marketing pages.
- Generic AI or neuroscience claims.
- Gamified music-learning imagery.
- Dense legal pages with weak contrast or narrow mobile layouts.
- Decorative card grids that make simple information harder to scan.

## Design Principles

- Put trust and clarity ahead of persuasion.
- Let Gradus feel like a working musician's tool.
- Use the established app identity consistently.
- Keep every important App Store document reachable in one tap.
- Make legal and support content comfortable to read on any screen.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, semantic HTML, visible keyboard focus, reduced
motion support, large touch targets, and layouts that remain readable at 200%
zoom.
